# Using Pins

Pinning keeps an important memo at the top of your timeline, whatever date you are looking at. It suits shopping lists, tasks in progress, and anything you want in sight every time you open Wrot.
Pin Later does the same thing on a delay: instead of pinning now, the memo is pinned on a day you choose.
This page explains both, and how the limits work.

---

## Pin

Choose **"Pin"** from a post's menu and the memo is fixed to the top of the timeline.

- It stays visible whatever date you have open, including memos that live in another day's note.
- A pin mark appears in the bottom-right corner of the card.
- The memo leaves its own day's timeline, so the same memo never shows up twice.
- To take it down, choose **"Unpin"** from the same menu.

A pinned memo cannot be deleted. Unpin it first if you want it gone.

---

## Pin Later

Instead of pinning now, the memo is pinned on a day you choose.

### Choosing a day

Choose **"Pin Later"** from a post's menu and a day picker opens.

- On desktop, a calendar.
- On phones and tablets, three dials for year, month and day, rising from the bottom of the screen.

Only days from tomorrow onwards can be chosen. To pin a memo today, use "Pin" instead.

To change the day, take it off and set it again.

### Setting one while you write

The clock button in the post form's toolbar does the same thing, for when you already know while writing that a post should come back later.

1. Press the clock button and choose a day.
2. The button stays lit. That is how you know a day is set.
3. Post as usual, and the day is applied to the post that was just created.

Press the lit button again to take the day back off. Hovering the button shows the day you chose.

The button is out of reach while you are editing an existing post: set that post's day from its own menu instead.

### While it waits

Until the day arrives the memo stays in its own day's timeline. It is not pinned yet.

- A clock mark appears in the bottom-right corner of the card, where the pin mark would be.
- As with a pin, the memo cannot be deleted.

### When the day arrives

The memo is pinned, and sits at the top of the timeline.

- The mark becomes a filled pin. Memos pinned normally keep the outlined pin, so the two are easy to tell apart.
- It sits below the memos pinned normally.
- It stays pinned after the day has passed, until you take it down.
- Take it down with **"Unpin"**, the same as any other pin.

Note that a memo does not appear the moment the date changes while Wrot is open. It arrives the next time you come back to Obsidian, or when you add a post.

---

## Limits

**"Pin Limit"** in the Advanced section of the settings offers 1, 3, or 5.

**"Pin" and "Pin Later" are counted separately.** With a limit of 3, you can hold 3 pins and 3 Pin Later memos.

- Once a limit is reached, that menu item is greyed out and a line below it says so.
- The clock button in the post form is also out of reach while the Pin Later limit is reached.
- A day set while writing claims its place straight away, so the allowance cannot fill up before you finish the post.
- Lowering the limit takes down anything over it, in each of the two allowances.
- A memo that arrived through Pin Later keeps using a Pin Later place afterwards. It never eats into the places for normal pins.

## Troubleshooting

### The day arrived but the memo has not been pinned

- Nothing happens at the moment the date changes. It catches up the next time you come back to Obsidian, or when you add a post.

### Pin Later cannot be chosen

- Check whether the Pin Later limit has been reached.
- Check whether the memo is already pinned or already has a day set. A memo cannot have both.
- It cannot be chosen while that memo is being edited. Finish or cancel the edit first.

### A memo cannot be deleted

- Memos that are pinned, or that have a day set, cannot be deleted. Take the pin down first.
- A tag rule with "Protect from deletion" also blocks deletion.
